.. _writing-precisely.conv.274:

------------------------------------------------------------
Expected and Provided Terms
------------------------------------------------------------

.. highlights:: Purpose

   This convention defines |this|. It is a method for
   evaluating a text by what it actually explains: the text
   is analyzed through its terms, each of which is either an
   expected term or a provided term. All other definitions
   build on this distinction.


Definitions
============================================================

.. glossary::
   :sorted:

   Reference (conv. 274)

      ~ is a word or phrase that points to a term or a
      definition elsewhere in the text without fully
      qualifying it.

      .. seealso::

         - :term:`Term (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Text (conv. 274)`


   Text (conv. 274)

      ~ is information structured by connecting terms for
      the purpose of encoding knowledge.

      .. seealso::

         - :term:`Term (conv. 274)`

   Unverified term (conv. 274)

      ~ is a word, phrase, or statement found in text before
      it is confirmed to be an expected term, a provided
      term, or a pseudo-term.

      .. seealso::

         - :term:`Expected term (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Provided term (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Pseudo-term (conv. 274)`


   Pseudo-term (conv. 274)

      ~ is a word, phrase, or statement used as a term while
      its definition is neither provided by the author nor
      known to the reader.

      .. seealso::

         - :term:`Expected term (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Insider term (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Provided term (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Term (conv. 274)`


   Argument (conv. 274)

      ~ is an elaboration whose statements are connected
      through reasoning to support its claim.

      .. seealso::

         - :term:`Claim (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Elaboration (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Enumeration of features (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Statement (conv. 274)`


   Balancing (conv. 274)

      ~ is organizing text in such a way that those parts
      that are recognized to be on the same level
      (structurally or semantically) must carry a comparable
      degree of detail.

      .. seealso::

         - :term:`Claim (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Elaboration (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Expected term (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Text (conv. 274)`


   Broader substitute (conv. 274)

      ~ is a broader term used where its narrower term
      already exists in the text: a term more general in
      meaning replaces a more specific term.

      .. seealso::

         - :term:`Broader term (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Derived term (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Narrower term (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Term variant (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Text (conv. 274)`


   Broader term (conv. 274)

      ~ is a term whose meaning fully includes the meaning
      of another term.

      .. seealso::

         - :term:`Broader substitute (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Narrower term (conv. 274)`



   Claim (conv. 274)

      ~ is a statement made of expected terms that the
      author chooses to explain.

      .. seealso::

         - :term:`Argument (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Balancing (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Elaboration (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Provided term (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Statement (conv. 274)`



   Dangling reference (conv. 274)

      ~ is a reference to something the text has never
      introduced.

      .. seealso::

         - :term:`Definition by reference (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Reference (conv. 274)`


   Author's privilege (conv. 274)

      ~ is a principle of trusting the author in their
      judgement about the terms they define. ~ does not
      apply to expected terms.

      .. seealso::

         - :term:`Term (conv. 274)`

   Declared list (conv. 274)

      ~ is a list introduced by an expected term that states
      the list is complete. By this convention, ~ defines
      the term it follows based on author's privilege.

      .. seealso::

         - :term:`Author's privilege (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Definition (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Illustration (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Unqualified list (conv. 274)`



   Defining category (conv. 274)

      ~ is the more general term into which a definition
      places the term it defines.

      .. seealso::

         - :term:`Definition (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Distinguishing feature (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Inflated category (conv. 274)`


   Definition (conv. 274)

      ~ relates a term to other terms so that the reader can
      tell what the term is. In a text, ~ takes one of four
      forms:

      1. a defining category with a distinguishing feature
      2. an elaboration, which defines a claim
      3. a definition by reference
      4. a declared list

      A partial definition is a weak form that may be used
      in contexts where a general idea of what the term
      represents would suffice for the purpose of the text.

      .. important::

         An illustration and an unqualified list are not
         definitions.

      .. seealso::

         - :term:`Claim (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Declared list (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Defining category (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Definition by reference (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Distinguishing feature (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Elaboration (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Partial definition (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Provided term (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Text (conv. 274)`


   Definition by reference (conv. 274)

      ~ is a definition that sends the reader to a
      definition elsewhere, either explicitly, pointing to a
      specific section, or implicitly, by using a broader
      reference which can be resolved unambiguously. ~ is
      valid only if its target is within the reader's reach,
      is specific, and is a definition whose terms the
      reader already has at the point of the reference.

      .. seealso::

         - :term:`Broader substitute (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Dangling reference (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Definition (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Promised and alien reference (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Promised and vague reference (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Reference (conv. 274)`


   Derived term (conv. 274)

      ~ is an expected term that enters the verified term
      catalog as a part of another term without being
      introduced by itself.


      .. seealso::

         - :term:`Broader substitute (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Verified term catalog (conv. 274)`


   Distinguishing feature (conv. 274)

      ~ is the part of a definition that separates the term
      being defined from the other terms of its defining
      category within the text. According to the author's
      privilege, ~ is sufficient if the term cannot be
      confused with another term in the text.

      .. seealso::

         - :term:`Defining category (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Definition (conv. 274)`



   Elaboration (conv. 274)

      ~ is a set of connected statements that support a
      claim.

      .. seealso::

         - :term:`Argument (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Claim (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Definition (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Enumeration of features (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Statement (conv. 274)`


   Enumeration of features (conv. 274)

      ~ is an elaboration that supports its claim by listing
      features of what the claim is about. The strength of
      the support depends on what the list contains.

      .. important::

         ~ is a list. Without an expected term introducing
         it, ~ is also an unqualified list.

      .. seealso::

         - :term:`Argument (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Author's privilege (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Elaboration (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Unqualified list (conv. 274)`


   Expected term (conv. 274)

      ~ is a term the text uses without explanation because
      the reader is *expected* to know it.

      .. seealso::

         - :term:`Balancing (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Insider term (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Misused expected term (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Provided term (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Term (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Term association (conv. 274)`


   Hedge (conv. 274)

      ~ is a word or phrase that softens the commitment of a
      statement or a relation, such as *usually*, *should*,
      *primarily*, or *for* as in "model for cybersecurity".

      .. important::

         A word such as "usually" that introduces the
         features of a term marks a partial definition,
         whether or not it is also a ~.

      .. seealso::

         - :term:`Partial definition (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Relation (conv. 274)`



   Illustration (conv. 274)

      ~ is a list introduced by an expected term to
      present examples of a term. ~ does not define the
      term.

      .. seealso::

         - :term:`Declared list (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Definition (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Unqualified list (conv. 274)`



   Incidental term (conv. 274)

      ~ is a provided term the text could do without:
      removing it loses nothing, however often it is used.

      .. seealso::

         - :term:`Provided term (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Redundant term (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Term (conv. 274)`


   Inflated category (conv. 274)

      ~ is an oversized defining category, such as "paradigm" for a model.

      .. seealso::

         - :term:`Defining category (conv. 274)`


   Insider term (conv. 274)

      ~ is a pseudo-term whose definition is settled by the
      shared context of the author's group.

      .. seealso::

         - :term:`Expected term (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Provided term (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Pseudo-term (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Term (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Unqualified list (conv. 274)`



   Misused expected term (conv. 274)

      ~ is an expected term used in a meaning that
      contradicts the meaning the reader already knows, such
      as "model" used for "approach".

      Being an expected term, ~ is not within author's
      privilege.

      .. seealso::

         - :term:`Author's privilege (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Expected term (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Term variant (conv. 274)`


   Narrower term (conv. 274)

      ~ is a term whose meaning is fully included in the
      meaning of its broader term.

      .. seealso::

         - :term:`Broader substitute (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Broader term (conv. 274)`


   Partial definition (conv. 274)

      ~ is a definition that states the common or essential
      features of a term and includes a hedge that
      effectively reduces the number of situations where
      this definition applies. ~ is weaker than the other
      forms of definition, but gives the reader enough to
      understand what the term is about according to the
      author's privilege.

      .. seealso::

         - :term:`Author's privilege (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Definition (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Hedge (conv. 274)`


   Promised and alien reference (conv. 274)

      ~ is a definition by reference whose target is a
      definition built on terms the reader does not have at
      the point of the reference.

      .. seealso::

         - :term:`Definition by reference (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Promised and vague reference (conv. 274)`


   Promised and vague reference (conv. 274)

      ~ is a definition by reference whose target is not a
      definition in any of its forms, or does not exist.

      .. seealso::

         - :term:`Definition by reference (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Promised and alien reference (conv. 274)`



   Provided term (conv. 274)

      ~ is a term the author defines in the text via the
      recognized definition forms.

      .. note::

         A claim is a ~.

      .. seealso::

         - :term:`Claim (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Definition (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Expected term (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Term (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Term association (conv. 274)`


   Redundant term (conv. 274)

      ~ is a term introduced but not used as introduced,
      such as an abbreviation that does not replace the full
      form in the rest of the text.

      .. seealso::

         - :term:`Incidental term (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Term variant (conv. 274)`


   Relation (conv. 274)

      ~ is a connection between two terms, or between a term
      and a statement, named by an expected term.

      .. important::

         A ~ serves the verified term catalog: analyzing ~s reveals
         terms that are missing or misused. The name of a ~
         can itself be a hedge or a misused expected term.

      .. seealso::

         - :term:`Hedge (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Statement (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Verified term catalog (conv. 274)`


   Statement (conv. 274)

      ~ is a fragment of text, a sentence or clause,
      considered as the smallest context when retrieving
      unverified terms for the unabridged term register.

      .. seealso::

         - :term:`Claim (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Unabridged term register (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Unverified term (conv. 274)`



   Term (conv. 274)

      ~ is a word, phrase, or statement associated with a
      definition provided by the author or known to the
      reader.

      .. admonition:: Reasoning

         ~s are the best means available for explaining
         something. Without them, an explanation becomes
         casual, and different readers take away different
         ideas. This convention uses ~ more broadly than a
         dictionary does: it requires no branch of study and
         no concept, and it is not limited to nouns.

      .. seealso::

         - :term:`Claim (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Definition (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Expected term (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Insider term (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Provided term (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Verified term catalog (conv. 274)`


   Term association (conv. 274)

      ~ is the label a term receives in the verified term
      catalog: expected term or provided term.

      .. seealso::

         - :term:`Expected term (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Provided term (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Verified term catalog (conv. 274)`


   Unabridged term register (conv. 274)

      ~ is the record of everything retrieved from a text
      as a possible term: unverified terms, terms, and
      pseudo-terms.

      .. seealso::

         - :term:`Pseudo-term (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Unverified term (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Verified term catalog (conv. 274)`

   Verified term catalog (conv. 274)

      ~ is the record of the terms that have passed
      verification, each with its term association.

      .. seealso::

         - :term:`Derived term (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Statement (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Term association (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Unabridged term register (conv. 274)`


   Term variant (conv. 274)

      ~ is a different wording used for an existing term
      that shares its defining category.

      ~ is acceptable if it is introduced and then used
      consistently.

      .. seealso::

         - :term:`Broader substitute (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Misused expected term (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Redundant term (conv. 274)`


   Unqualified list (conv. 274)

      ~ is a sequence that includes expected terms, provided terms, or
      both, that has no expected term introducing it. The
      sequence may mix expected and provided terms as long as
      its provided terms are not insider terms.

      .. important::

         Regardless of author's privilege, a term that
         appears in more than one list requires every such
         list to be qualified.

      .. seealso::

         - :term:`Declared list (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Illustration (conv. 274)`
         - :term:`Insider term (conv. 274)`
