Definitions
============================================================

.. glossary::
   :sorted:

   Left term

      A sequence of characters from the beginning of the line up to the secondary separator

   Pair

      A combination of the left term, secondary separator, and the right term

   Primary separator

      A symbol that marks the end of a pair

   Right term
   
      A sequnce of characters from the secondary separator up to the primary separator

   Secondary separator

      ~ is a symbol that separates the left term from the right term

   Section

      ~ is a combination of the left term and the secondary separator

   Value

      ~ is a combination of the right term and the primary separator

   Sequence
   
      ~ is a combination of values.
