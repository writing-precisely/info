------------------------------------------------------------
Perspectives
------------------------------------------------------------

.. highlights::

   `Perspectives` are ideas, observations, or conclusions worth
   sharing. These are short essays that do not necessarily have
   a solid argumentation. In most cases, I don't even attempt
   to prove I am right.
