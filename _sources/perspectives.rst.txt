------------------------------------------------------------
Perspectives
------------------------------------------------------------

Perspectives are ideas, observations, or conclusions that I
share because I believe they can be interesting. These are
short essays that do not necessarily have a solid
argumentation. In most cases, I don't even attempt to prove
I am right.
