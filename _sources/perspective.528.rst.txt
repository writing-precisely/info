.. _writing-precisely.perspective.528:

------------------------------------------------------------
Human-AI Interface
------------------------------------------------------------

User interface is about control. The right amount of
control.  It is granted to users to allow software to serve
its purpose properly. A system of considerable complexity
must provide an abundant number of ways to interact with
it. This is how sophisticated capabilities are made
available.

Artificial intelligence was introduced to the world at large
through quite a modest user interface: a prompt area to "ask
anything you want", the response area for intelligent
responses, and simple ways to organize conversations. This
contradicts the expectation based on experience of using
modern software.

.. _writing-precisely.perspective.528-23:

It Is Unique
============================================================

As soon as we realize that artificial intelligence is a
unique phenomenon that functions on principles totally
different from what constitutes conventional software, we
shatter not only the idea of user interface but also the
idea of control itself.

The reason the user interface for AI is so simple is not
that the underlying system is simple. The complexity exists
and lives elsewhere. We can use natural language. Not highly
formal expressions, such as in programming languages, that
you have to learn specifically. Everyday language with all
its irregularity, ambiguity, and vagueness will suffice. AI
will understand you and respond by answering hard questions,
analyzing complex ideas, even constructing software along
with a sophisticated user interface.

.. _writing-precisely.perspective.528-93:

It Understands Us
============================================================

The scenario of constructing software or any other system is
a special case. Although we don't have to be as formal as in
programming languages, we must use clear and unambiguous
language. What if we don't? The system won't be
built? Surprisingly, it is quite the opposite: *AI always
delivers*. It just won't be the expected result.

OK, no problem: clear and unambiguous. Got it.

In fact, the *clear and unambiguous* is a serious
challenge. Because it is not what *we consider clear and
unambiguous*. It is what *is translatable into a set of
instructions that can be interpreted in one way
only*. Since AI always delivers, our control is getting
looser at every point where there is another way to see
the tiniest bit of our request. We can't figure out each
such point ourselves.

.. _writing-precisely.perspective.528-48:

We Must Be Specific
============================================================

The problem of making instructions effective is well known
and widely discussed. There are several ways to address it:
properly constructed prompts, curated examples,
specification driven development, skills, context
management, and so on. And they work. What is difficult
about any solution is to apply these methods to a growing
system with all the uncertainty that comes with changes.

We must discipline ourselves to use the language as a tool
and apply a well defined protocol for controlling the AI by
restricting it to a specific role, by analyzing and giving
feedback, and by seeking ambiguity and vagueness across
instructions. The discipline works as long as the language
is precise, AI stays within the boundaries of the assigned
role, the feedback is complete and trustworthy, and all
instances of *there is more than one way to see it* are
reported.

Here, we must remember something that any AI client
interface provides: *AI can make mistakes.* And it will if
there is no method that warrants feedback trustworthiness
and completeness of the instruction problems
report. Mistakes in a thoroughly crafted set of instructions
are much harder to find than in a sloppy request.

The harsh truth is that we cannot control AI doing its job.

.. _writing-precisely.perspective.528-64:

We Control It
============================================================

Our interaction with artificial intelligence is of a unique
nature. Unprecedented. The forms of making effective
instructions have limitations because they have grown from
human-to-human interactions. Although artificial
intelligence is great at reasoning, it is not identical
to a human: it connects the dots differently.
