------------------------------------------------------------
Conventions
------------------------------------------------------------

`Conventions` are definitions of information tools.

Compliance with a proposition

A convention complies with one or more propositions. A compliant convention:

- must serve every Context item of the proposition with at least one method;
- must not contradict any Assumption of the proposition in its definitions or methods;
- may provide definitions and methods beyond what the proposition requires. This additional value is what distinguishes conventions that comply with the same proposition.

A convention's definitions and methods together form the conceptual design.
